Virro, Where waste is never wasted.

Brand strategy, product names, and visual identity for Virro. The concept of the composting toilet is not new, but the Virro team's approach to it is. We worked with them to understand their unique POV and translate it into a compelling identity that sets them apart from the legacy sanitation players in the space.

  • Agency: Diver
  • Strategy: John Edmonds
  • Creative Direction & Design: Nick Johnston

Momday, The strength that carries.

Momday is a fitness app for pregnant and postpartum women. They had outgrown their existing app and came to Input Logic to design new features, bring their new brand into the product, and build it. I worked with their team to translate the brand for digital, extending the brand standards and setting a clear direction for both the website and product UI. Alongside the UI work, we defined a motion language that carried through to their launch marketing materials.

  • Agency: Input Logic
  • Brand Strategy: Frank Strategy
  • Brand Design: Talia Cohen
  • UX: Nelson Reid
  • Product Design: Nick Johnston

Avo Aero, The Freedom of Flight.

Avo Aero has an ambitious goal: to make eVTOL vehicles commonplace in driveways across the US. When the founder approached Diver, he was building small-scale prototypes and preparing to raise a major funding round, but he had no brand. He understood the power of good design and wanted to look credible to investors from day one. We worked with him to craft a clear, ownable brand strategy, a visual identity, a website, and a pitch deck written in the brand's voice to help him secure his first round of funding.

  • Agency: Diver
  • Brand Strategy: John Edmonds
  • Creative Direction & Design: Nick Johnston

Backland, Answer The Call.

What started as a typography exercise turned into a full visual identity update. Backland first approached me to design title typography for a few films they were producing, but it became clear early on that a full identity refresh would help support the new type direction. I updated their visual identity and gave them a new toolkit for creating marketing materials. The result cuts through a category crowded with loud, condensed typefaces and aggressive design. It feels technical, rugged, and precise, just like the products they build.

  • Agency: Diver
  • Creative Direction & Design: Nick Johnston

We Who Wander, Curated for the Adventurous.

Jack approached me to create a brand identity for his new premium hiking tour company along Australia's Great Ocean Walk. Together we created an identity that stands apart from the other operators in the area. At its heart is a set of landscape illustrations by Sebastian Abboud, used on everything from merch to the website. In a second phase, we designed and developed a custom site where guests can book their trip and their stay at Jack's hotel. This project is my favourite kind of work: designing everything from the brand through to every digital touchpoint of the business.

  • Agency: Diver
  • Illustration: Sebastian Abboud
  • Website Development: Input Logic
  • Creative Direction & Design: Nick Johnston

Clear Falls, Where Time Slows.

A few years ago, I had the chance to work on a couple of projects for author James Clear, both tied to his family property in Hocking Hills, Ohio, which he named Clear Falls. James hired me to create a suite of logos, but what I presented was a full visual identity: typography, colours, website, and merch. His aha moment wasn't seeing the logo on a letterhead or etched into the metal of the front gate. It was seeing every other touchpoint considered, from the website all the way down to a pattern for wallpaper and napkins.

  • Creative Direction & Design: Nick Johnston
  • Motion Design: Andrew Embury

Kind Words

Why work with me?

Most brand projects involve a handoff. A brand designer creates the identity, then a UI designer interprets it for the product. Things shift. Details get lost. The brand you approved and the interface your users experience don't quite match. I eliminate that gap. The result is a brand that feels cohesive everywhere it shows up.
